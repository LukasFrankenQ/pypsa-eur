Vendored third-party primary sources.

The files under data/custom/sources/ are unmodified copies of primary data
published by third parties (Redeia/REE, MITECO, RTE, Ember, JRC, ERSE, PyPSA
technology-data, and others). The hourly, daily and monthly series in
data/custom/ that the scripts/retrieve_*.py scripts take from OMIE, RTE, REN,
MIBGAS, Instrat and Energy-Charts change only the format of the providers' data.
Both are kept for reproducibility only and remain under their providers' own
licenses and terms of use.

The origin, retrieval date, license and SHA-256 checksum of every vendored file
are registered in doc/data_sources.md.
